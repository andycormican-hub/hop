HOP

Move treasury cash between spendable dollars and earning dollars. Refuse the hop if it cannot settle.

Rule sheet

  1. Keep at least USDC spendable at all times.

  2. Park idle cash in

  3. Have cash back hours before any payment is due.

  4. If that deadline falls on a weekend,

  5. Allow only when the feed reports regular. Any other reading queues for the next open or refuses. This rule is fixed.
